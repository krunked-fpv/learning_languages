# WANDERLING 2.0: THE GRAND ATLAS OF LIVING TONGUES (A1–C2)

## 1. World Premise & Creative Aesthetic
**Wanderling 2.0** blends *Nintendo tactile toy-craft* with *Pixar storybook illustration*. Young explorers captain the **Starlight Compass** expedition alongside **Pip the Polyglot Starling** (a round, expressive cobalt-and-gold songbird explorer wearing an oversized brass compass medallion) to awaken the **Living Phrasebook** by mastering **16 living languages** from **Beginner (`A1`) to Fluent Mastery (`C1–C2`)** across Latin, Arabic, Cyrillic, Devanagari, Bengali, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese scripts—across **256 themed cultural units**, **1,792+ adventure map nodes** (plus dynamic Tier Placement Checkpoints), and **2,400+ Living Phrasebook stickers**.

### 5-Tier CEFR Scaffolding (`A1`–`C2`)
- **`A1` — Sprout (Units 1–4, `guided`):** Always-visible phonetic/romanized clues and tap-hint glosses covering greetings, cafe treats, animals, family, and survival phrases.
- **`A2` — Trailblazer (Units 5–8, `guided`):** Expeditions, music & hobbies, cozy home & clothes, and everyday conversations.
- **`B1` — Storyweaver (Units 9–11, `bridge`):** Native-script-first presentation with 1-tap phonetic/romanized peeks across past stories, future dreams, and empathy connectors.
- **`B2` — Wayfinder Diplomat (Units 12–14, `bridge`):** Science curiosity, festivals, folklore, register contrasts, and Context Cloze nuance spotlights.
- **`C1+` (`C1_C2`) — Polyglot Laureate (Units 15–16, `fluent`):** Compact multi-clause word banks, native idioms & proverbs, Keyboard Free-Recall typing, and on-demand **Tier Placement Checkpoints** (`A2` through `C1+`).

## 2. Core UX, Craft & Platform Mandates
- **Cross-Platform Custom Vector Flags:** Windows renders Unicode country-flag emojis as plain two-letter text (`ES`, `FR`, `DE`, `IT`, `PT`, `EN`, `ZH`, `JA`, `HI`, `BN`, `AR`, `ARZ`, `RU`, `ID`, `VI`, `SW`). Every language flag across the 16 base languages and all 7 After-Hours split-emblem crests in the top HUD, profile cards, and Phrasebook MUST be rendered via crisp inline SVG (`FlagIcon.tsx`), with zero regional-indicator Unicode characters (`U+1F1E6–U+1F1FF`) anywhere in code, data, or documentation.
- **Tactile 3D-Bevel Toy-Craft Physics & 15 Color Palettes:** Interactive cards, word-bank tiles, and primary action buttons use chunky `border-bottom-width: 4px` (or `5px` for primary CTAs) with spring `translateY(2px)` press feedback, 50 cosmetics across 7 slots (`hat`, `glasses`, `cape`, `aura`, `prop`, `pet`, `theme`), 10 avatars, and 15 palettes (Default *Storybook Parchment* plus 14 unlockable light and dark themes) maintaining readable ink contrast across light/dark surfaces.
- **Strict `100dvh` Full-Viewport Game Console:** Active lessons and the 9-cabinet proficiency-scaled Arcade (*Sky-Catcher Fiesta*, *Cosmic Meteor Blaster*, *Gelato & Pastry Tower*, *Constellation Memory Twins*, *Alpine Express Switcher*, *Beat Kitchen Rhythm*, *Polyglot Café Order Rush*, *Starlight Bridge Cloze Builder*, and secret *Starlight Critter Choir*) frame cleanly inside `100dvh` across Desktop (`1440x900`, `1024x768`), Tablet (`768x1024`), and Mobile (`390x844`, `375x667`) with zero mid-lesson outer page scrolling (`document.documentElement.scrollHeight <= window.innerHeight`).
- **Kid-Safe Warm Hospitality & Quarantine:** Every base café, bakery, and travel dialogue celebrates kid-loved treats (hot chocolate, croissants, churros, pretzels, orange juice, mango gelato, fresh apples)—never alcohol or adult bar themes, and never harsh red buzzers. Default kid explorers (**Leo** `kid-leo` and **Maya** `kid-maya`) are permanently quarantined to the 16 base languages.
- **Multi-Script Accent-Forgiving + Teachable Grading:** Learners typing or selecting base ASCII letters (`nino`, `garcon`, `fur`, `strasse`, `caffe`, stripped Vietnamese tones, unvocalized Arabic, Devanagari/Bengali base forms, or Pinyin/Romaji romanizations) pass with **zero heart penalty** while triggering a celebratory golden **Accent Spotlight!** callout teaching the exact diacritics (`ñ`, `ç`, `ü`, `ß`, `è/à`, tone marks, or script nuancing).
- **Zero-Stall Offline Speech & Audio (`S2-004`):** Synthesizes 24 acoustic effects natively via Web Audio (`AudioContext`), supports `0.95x` Normal / `0.65x` Slow Turtle / `1.12x` Fluent Web Speech across 16 base locales (`en-US`/`en` for After-Hours) with a `4500ms` hung-utterance watchdog that cancels stalled OS utterances, fires non-blocking marimba syllable cadence playback (`playPhoneticCadence`), increments `watchdogRescueCount` (`SpeakOutcome.watchdogTriggered = true`), and displays a `"Marimba Phonetic Guide Active!"` toast inside `LessonArena` so lessons never stall.
- **Offline-Installable GitHub Pages PWA (`S2-001`, `S2-003`):** Strictly relative `./` routing (`base: './'`), pure-Node anti-aliased RGBA PNG icons (`192x192`, `512x512`, maskable variants, and `180x180` Apple touch icon), self-hosted `.woff2` fonts (`@fontsource-variable/fredoka` and `@fontsource-variable/nunito`, zero external CDNs), per-language dynamic base-course chunking (`es` synchronous + lazy `fr`..`sw`) enforced by a strict `<= 500 KiB` per-JS-chunk build gate, network-first Service Worker (`./sw.js`, `wanderling-cache-v8`) with deduplicated `PRECACHE_URLS` injected post-build alongside `dist/sw-precache-manifest.json` (`scripts/inject-sw-precache.mjs`), `caches.delete` stale-cache purging, Profile Vault 1-tap **Offline Atlas Pack** pre-downloading (`prefetchAllOfflinePacks`), subpath offline fallback, and multi-kid local vault (`wanderling.vault.v1`) with schema-validated JSON export/import and legacy save migration.

## 4. Sprint 2 Upgrades (`S2-001` – `S2-007`)
- **`S2-001` — Per-Language Dynamic Chunking & `<= 500 KiB` Guard:** Splits non-Spanish base courses (`fr`..`sw`) into dynamic per-language chunks (`baseRegistry.ts`, `baseCoursesLoader.prod.ts` for build and `baseCoursesLoader.test.ts` for synchronous Vitest runs), surfaces `BaseTrackLoadingCard` with retry + Español fallback across Map/Phrasebook/Arcade, and enforces `<= 500 * 1024` bytes per `.js`/`.mjs` chunk in `scripts/verify-build-artifacts.mjs`.
- **`S2-002` — Romanized Tone-Coaching Free-Recall:** `resolveFreeRecallHint` and `FreeRecallHintBar` detect Latin/romanized typing on non-Latin courses (`isRomanizedMode`, **"Romanized Syllable Guide"**) and render non-blocking **Tone & Diacritic Coaching** (`toneCoaching`, zero heart penalty) when NFD-stripped tokens match while exact diacritics or tones differ.
- **`S2-003` — `wanderling-cache-v8` Full-Asset SW Precache Manifest & Offline Pack Downloader:** `scripts/inject-sw-precache.mjs` injects deduplicated core shell + sorted `./assets/*.js|mjs|css|woff2` entries into `dist/sw.js` and generates `dist/sw-precache-manifest.json`, verified by `scripts/verify-build-artifacts.mjs` and paired with Profile Vault's 1-tap **Offline Atlas Pack** downloader (`prefetchAllOfflinePacks`).
- **`S2-004` — `4500ms` Audio Watchdog Marimba Rescue:** Stuck OS speech synthesis settles cleanly at `4500ms` with `watchdogTriggered: true`, non-blocking `playPhoneticCadence`, incremented `watchdogRescueCount`, and `"Marimba Phonetic Guide Active!"` toast feedback.
- **`S2-005` — Mid-Lesson Study Break Panic Switch:** `LessonArena` mounts a compact **`Study Break`** button strictly when `isAdultLanguage(language)` is true, stopping speech and disarming Adult Mode back to `lastBaseLanguage` in 1 tap.
- **`S2-006` — Elision/Proclitic Stem Lookup & Virtual Script Drawer:** `lookupGlossaryEntry` resolves French/Italian apostrophe elisions and Arabic bound proclitics via stem synthesis, while `VirtualScriptDrawer.tsx` (`[Script Keys]`, collapsed by default) lets Free-Recall learners insert Pinyin, Kana, Arabic, Devanagari, Bengali, Cyrillic, Vietnamese, and Latin helper characters at cursor position inside `100dvh`.
- **`S2-007` — Adaptive SRS Memory Quests & 2P Family Co-Op Arcade:** Per-card `vocabMastery` powers daily **Starlight Memory Quests** (`buildSrsReviewQuestNode`, `scoreSrsCandidates`, `computeUpdatedVocabMastery`) from `AdventureMap` without inflating `lessonsCompleted`, while `ArcadeHub` supports 2-player pass-and-play **Family Co-Op** across all 9 cabinets with `recordCoopArcadeSession` and strict Kid-Safety Quarantine.
